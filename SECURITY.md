# Security and Responsible Research

This repository is designed for public-source research and synthetic educational exercises. It does not provide an implementation for covertly modifying third-party repositories, poisoning real model artifacts, persisting hidden changes, or disrupting live training jobs.

Please do not submit real credentials, private internal logs, personal information, or confidential company data. If you have permission to analyze original artifacts, preserve them read-only, document provenance, and follow the artifact owner's disclosure and privacy requirements.
